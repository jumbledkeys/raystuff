Title: Switch On, Turn Off - Investigating Electricity and Water Waste at School

Background

A. What is the problem?

During our observation around the school, we noticed two common phenomena: lights and electricity remain on even when no one is inside the classroom, and water faucets are often left open or dripping after use. This happens in classrooms, bathrooms, and hallways.

This is a problem because it causes a waste of electricity and water, which are important resources. Wasting electricity increases school costs and energy consumption, while wasting water reduces clean water availability.

This affects everyone around us — students, teachers, and the school environment. Since these small habits happen every day, we decided to investigate how much electricity and water is being wasted and why people are unaware of it.

Topic B

B. Driving Question — What Do We Want to Find Out?

  1. How often is the aircon left powered on in an empty class?
  2. How often does our group notice the open sink being left on?
Topic C1

C1. PHENOMENON — What Did We See?

  1. What did we see? We saw AC still ON in the music room even though the room was empty, and we saw water sinks broken and left open by students, dripping continuously.
  2. What did we notice? AC still on almost everyday in the music room even when the light is off and the room is empty; water sink broken and keeping dripping; sink left open by students after washing hands and not closed tightly.
  3. What was unusual? The music room was empty and dark (light off) but the AC was still running and cold almost everyday. At the same time, sinks were wasting water all day because they were broken or left open. This waste happens because of careless habits, not active usage.
