Title: Switch On, Turn Off - Investigating Electricity and Water Waste at School

Chapter F

F. Conclusion

What Did We Learn?

Investigation Summary

Final Reflections & Key Findings

Problems Investigated: ACs left running in empty music rooms and bathroom sinks dripping or left open continuously.

Core Evidence: Daily observation recorded constant AC operation in empty rooms with lights turned off, along with non-stop tap leaking in school restrooms.

Conclusion: These continuous habits create substantial electricity and water waste while causing slippery, unsafe bathroom conditions.

Proposed Solution: Integrating motion sensors with door reminder signs for AC units, alongside automatic infrared sensor faucets for bathroom sinks.

What We Learned: Small careless daily habits accumulate into major environmental and financial impacts. Implementing smart automation and sensors significantly boosts school sustainability.
