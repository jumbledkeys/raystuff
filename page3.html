Title: Switch On, Turn Off - Investigating Electricity and Water Waste at School

Chapters D & E

Data Analysis & Design Solution

Understanding the impact and engineering our proposed remedies.

D. DATA ANALYSIS

Why Does The Data Matter?

1. AC Left On in Empty Music Room:
We often found the AC left running in the music room even though lights were off and no one was inside. ACs convert electrical energy into thermal energy to cool air. When running in empty rooms, electricity is continually consumed without purpose, producing pure energy waste.

2. Broken and Open Sinks:
Continuous dripping from broken or unclosed taps wastes clean processed water that requires energy to purify and pump. This directly increases the school's water footprint and leaves bathroom floors wet and unsafe.

3. Analysis Conclusion:
Both problems illustrate how everyday human habits create large energy and water losses. Better habits and proactive maintenance directly preserve vital resources.

E. DESIGN SOLUTION

How Can We Solve the Problem?

1. Proposed Solution:
AC: Motion sensor ceiling module + "Turn Off AC & Lights" sign near the door.
Sink: Replacement with automatic infrared sensor faucets.

2. How It Works:
AC Motion Sensor: Scans room activity. If no motion is detected for 10–15 minutes, it cuts power automatically.
Sensor Faucet: Infrared detection activates water flow only when hands are present and shuts off immediately upon removal.

3. Rationale:
Removes reliance on human memory, overcoming daily forgetfulness and leaky manual taps.

4. Prototype Concept:
Conceptual cardboard model depicting music room ceiling motion sensors, reminder signs, sensor faucets, and flow logic:
No motion → AC OFF | Hands detected → Water ON | Hands away → Water OFF

5. Expected Impact:
Lower utility costs, reduced waste footprint, safer dry bathroom floors, and sustained automated energy saving.
